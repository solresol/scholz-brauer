# Hansen's Addition-Chain Construction in Lean 4 — v0.1.0

This software release formalises the general marked Hansen construction:
an accepted source with r additions and endpoint n gives an addition chain
for 2^n-1 with exactly n-1+r additions. The proof establishes allocation of
every required node and summand, successful sorted replay, and the exact count.

The principal results are `hansenLift_chain`, `hansenLift_certificate` and
`hansenLift_upper_bound` in `lean/ScholzBrauer/HansenLift.lean`.
`mersenne12509_length_le_12525` in `Example12509.lean` instantiates the theorem
at the independently checked 17-step source for 12509.

Lean also proves exclusion-certificate soundness, ell(7)=4, and
16 <= ell(12509) <= 17. Independent exhaustive computations establish
ell(12509)=17, but the exclusion through 16 additions has not been checked
by the Lean kernel. Consequently the complete Scholz instance at 12509
remains a computational result, not an end-to-end Lean theorem.
There is no claim of optimality for the Mersenne chain, a proof of the general
Scholz conjecture, or a new classical mathematical theorem. Priority for the
formalisation has not been established.

## Reproduction

Requirements: Git, Python >= 3.9, a C++17 compiler, and elan/Lake. From a fresh
checkout of this release, run:

```sh
cd lean
lake exe cache get
lake build
cd ..
python3 scripts/verify_integration.py --output results/release-local.json
```

The preliminary build lets the integration runner use its existing per-command
180-second bound even on a cold checkout. Choose a fresh report path. The
runner checks the stored certificates, bounded exhaustive searches, generated
fixture agreement, vendor hashes, Lean build and 111 named theorem dependency
audits. Only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are permitted.
These audits check dependencies of the named theorems; they do not certify
mathematical novelty or human interpretation of the statements.

Lean is pinned to 4.27.0, mathlib to
`a3a10db0e9d66acbebf76c5e6a135066525ac900`, with a committed dependency manifest.
The adapted formal-conjectures definitions are pinned to
`40e7c98697de6f66b8cbdbf641749ab39ed9c152`. The unproved upstream Scholz statement
is retained solely as an uncompiled reference; it is not imported or assumed.
The research reports record exact inputs, source checks and prior runtimes.
