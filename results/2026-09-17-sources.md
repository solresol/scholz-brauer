Read both actual source files. The addition-chain definition is a list starting
at 1, strictly increasing, whose noninitial entries are sums of entries in the
list. Positivity makes those summands earlier than their sum. The upstream
length is a natural-number infimum over admissible chain lengths. Its witness
lemma supplies an upper bound; the doubling lemma supplies a lower bound.

The upstream Scholz statement quantifies over positive natural numbers and
contains an open-problem placeholder. We retained it as an uncompiled reference,
not a dependency. The Apache-2.0 headers and licence are preserved. Five raw
source/configuration files were fetched at the pinned commit; their SHA-256
hashes are in `lean/vendor/formal-conjectures/provenance.json` and were rechecked.

Upstream uses Lean/mathlib 4.33.1. Local compatibility changes for 4.27.0 are
listed in `lean/README.md`; a byte comparison after these explicit transformations
confirmed that the mathematical definitions and proofs were preserved. Mathlib

numerical target is 12509. Its 17-step witness is kernel checked independently
of any literature claim about optimality. The proved lower bound is 14.
No Hansen predicate or Mersenne lift has yet been implemented. There was no
